        Why compute counts for the climate.

        Data centres and networks use a share of the world’s electricity that is small today but growing fast, mostly because of video and AI workloads. Where that power comes from decides the impact: the same task is far cleaner on a grid full of wind, solar and hydro than on one run mainly on coal.

        Sustainable computing is practical climate action: use less energy per task, run work where and when power is cleaner, and keep devices in use for longer.

        If you build software, start here.

        Send less data
        Compress images, use modern formats, lazy-load media and cache aggressively. Smaller pages use less energy on the server, the network and the device.
        Right-size the compute
        Use smaller models when they are good enough, batch jobs, and switch off idle servers and test environments.
        Run it where the grid is clean
        Choose cloud regions with low-carbon electricity and schedule flexible jobs for times when renewables are plentiful.
        Measure it
        Track energy and emissions the same way you track latency and cost, so improvements are visible.
